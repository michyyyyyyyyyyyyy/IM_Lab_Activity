Process

How it works at HCDC

1

Report a Lost or Found Item

Share a clear description, campus building, and photo so fellow Crusaders and staff can help identify it.

2

Browse and Identify Items

Search reports from Mabutas, Palma Gil, Sta. Ana, and Thibault halls, plus the library, chapel, canteen, and gymnasium. Sensitive details stay hidden until a claim is verified.

3

Claim and Pick Up on Campus

Describe unique features, wait for staff review, then recover approved items at the HCDC Security Desk.

About FINDIT

Built for Holy Cross of Davao College students, faculty, and staff.

FINDIT is a campus lost and found system. It keeps reports from classrooms, offices, and student areas organized, protects identifying details, and gives HCDC administrators a clear workflow for claims.
